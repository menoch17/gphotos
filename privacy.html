gphotos privacy policy

gphotos is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own photos to the owner's own Google Photos account.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Photos Library API only to upload media and to create and read the albums it created itself. It does not read, download or change any other photos in the account.

What it stores and shares

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's home server. No data from Google is collected, sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is sent to any service other than Google Photos.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page.
